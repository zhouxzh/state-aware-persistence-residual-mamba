Taken together, the currently completed optimized evidence supports three main observations.
First, Table~\ref{tab:gld-term-avg} shows that PMS-Mamba-PD is the strongest overall model across the combined baseline and Mamba comparison, followed by NS-Mamba-D and the best non-Mamba baselines.
Second, the horizon-range averages show a trade-off: NLinear remains strongest on the short-range average, while PMS-Mamba-PD leads the middle-range, long-range, and overall summaries.
Third, because the Mamba rows now use the same RMSSE definition and horizon grouping as the baseline rows, the comparison is direct: PMS-Mamba-PD reduces average RMSSE by 5.89\% against the strongest Optuna-tuned non-Mamba baseline on the full 12-horizon summary.

At the same time, the Mamba rows should be interpreted as the completed GLD Mamba-family search state rather than as a final complete ranking beyond GLD.
