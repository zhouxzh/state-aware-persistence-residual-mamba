NS-Mamba-D ranks 5th at 30 days, 4th at 120 days, 3rd at 180 days, and 4th at 360 days among all completed models.
On the long-horizon average over 120, 180, and 360 days, NS-Mamba-D ranks 4th overall with RMSE 26.74 and MAE 19.54.
It beats every shared locked-setting baseline and every other NS-Mamba diagnostic variant, but it trails the tuned imported PatchTST, NLinear, and TimesNet baselines.
This comparison predates the later Mamba-family hyperparameter optimization; after that search, optimized MS-Mamba-MD becomes the strongest Mamba-family result.
