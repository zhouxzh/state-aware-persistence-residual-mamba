The completed optimized imported sweep changes the comparison picture substantially.
NLinear is the strongest imported model on the short horizons from 1 to 14 days.
TimesNet is strongest at 30 and 90 days.
PatchTST is strongest from 60 days onward except for 90 days, and it is the best imported model overall under the completed optimized sweep.

The best lookback pattern also depends strongly on the model.
NLinear prefers very large windows from 192 to 384.
PatchTST prefers 16 on short horizons, then 256 to 320 at middle horizons, and 128 at 270 and 360 days.
TimesNet prefers 16 on short horizons and much larger windows at middle and long horizons.
Non-stationary Transformer still prefers mostly 16, which appears too short for strong long-horizon GLD performance.

Compared with the best locked-setting baseline at each completed horizon, the optimized imported baselines are substantially stronger except for DLinear.
For example, optimized NLinear reduces RMSE by 42.41\% at 1 day, optimized TimesNet reduces RMSE by 20.36\% at 90 days, optimized PatchTST reduces RMSE by 23.02\% at 120 days, and optimized PatchTST reduces RMSE by 15.51\% at 360 days.
DLinear is weaker than the best locked-setting baseline at every horizon, while Non-stationary Transformer is competitive at short horizons but no longer competitive with the strongest imported models once the full optimized sweep is complete.
