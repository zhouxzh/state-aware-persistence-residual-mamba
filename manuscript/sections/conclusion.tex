\section{Conclusion}

This work develops PMS-Mamba, a decomposition-aware multi-scale patch Mamba model for nonstationary financial level forecasting.
Its central design choice is to place the selective state-space scan over ordered temporal patches rather than over variable tokens alone.
The resulting representation preserves the normalized input through additive decomposition, pools temporal variation without amplifying a maximum-norm perturbation, and uses bounded fixed mean fusion across scales.

Selection-safe analysis of foreign exchange, Treasury yields, cross-asset prices, and cryptoassets documents material changes in local level, scale, volatility, and cross-series dependence.
These observations motivate the model but do not demonstrate forecast superiority.
The required next step is a recomputed, mechanism-oriented ablation study with matched capacity and a protected out-of-sample evaluation.
Until those results are complete, the manuscript makes no claim that PMS-Mamba improves financial forecasts.
