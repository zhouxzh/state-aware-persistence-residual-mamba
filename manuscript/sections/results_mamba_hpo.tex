After tuning, MS-Mamba-MD is the strongest Mamba-family model, with average RMSE 24.45 and average MAE 17.97 over the completed long diagnostic horizons.
This changes the cross-model ranking: the best optimized Mamba model is now stronger than optimized NLinear and TimesNet on the 120/180/360-day average.
However, optimized PatchTST remains the strongest model in this comparison, with average RMSE 23.29; the remaining relative RMSE gap for the best Mamba model is 4.96\%.
The residual gap to PatchTST is concentrated at 120 days (16.32 vs. 15.32), 180 days (21.47 vs. 21.23), 360 days (35.55 vs. 33.33).
