This track should be interpreted as a current completed imported-model reference set rather than a finalized global benchmark.

Within the currently completed imported runs, NLinear is strongest from 1 to 14 days, TimesNet is strongest at 30 and 90 days, and PatchTST is strongest from 60 days onward except at 90 days.
Among the completed imported models, PatchTST is therefore the strongest long-horizon reference model.

The best lookback pattern also depends strongly on the model.
NLinear prefers very large windows from 192 to 384.
PatchTST prefers 16 on short horizons, then 256 to 320 at middle horizons, and 128 at 270 and 360 days.
TimesNet prefers 16 on short horizons and much larger windows at middle and long horizons.
Non-stationary Transformer still prefers mostly 16, which appears too short for strong long-horizon GLD performance.

Compared with the best optimized directly comparable baseline at each completed horizon, the imported optimized baselines are substantially stronger except for DLinear.
For example, optimized NLinear reduces RMSE by 0.58\% at 1 day, optimized TimesNet reduces RMSE by 8.76\% at 90 days, optimized PatchTST reduces RMSE by 7.54\% at 120 days, and optimized PatchTST reduces RMSE by 8.57\% at 360 days.
DLinear is weaker than the best optimized directly comparable baseline at every horizon, while Non-stationary Transformer is competitive at short horizons but not with the strongest completed imported models at long horizons.
