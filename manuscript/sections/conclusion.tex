\section{Conclusion}

This work establishes a locked benchmark for evaluating Mamba-based forecasts of nonstationary financial levels.
Four financial panels yield paired univariate-input and multivariate-input single-target tasks, evaluated at six horizons against five learned baselines and four Mamba configurations.
The final design adds persistence, drift, ARIMA, and VAR-on-differences references, five neural seeds, paired S/MS analysis, moving-block bootstrap intervals, multiplicity adjustment, and prespecified volatility regimes.

Training/validation diagnostics show substantial nonstationarity and distribution shift, but they do not establish forecast superiority.
The 2023--2026 final period remains locked after validation-only selection was completed and audited.
Accordingly, the present conclusion is procedural rather than empirical: the study is positioned to test whether the proposed variants improve on strong financial references without reusing development outcomes, but that question remains unanswered in this manuscript version.
Any final conclusion will be generated only from the complete validated evidence matrix and will preserve unfavorable, null, and panel-specific findings.
