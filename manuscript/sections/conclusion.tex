\section{Conclusion}

This manuscript presents a structured path for nonstationarity-aware Mamba forecasting.
The completed GLD baseline study shows that S\_Mamba is the strongest short- and middle-horizon baseline, while iTransformer is the strongest long-horizon RMSE baseline under the shared locked setting.
The completed source-optimized imported sweep further shows that NLinear is strongest at short horizons and PatchTST is strongest overall among the tuned imported baselines.
The proposed NS-Mamba and MS-Mamba architecture families combine decomposition, multi-scale temporal modeling, and Mamba-based sequence processing around an S\_Mamba backbone.
The first diagnostic ablation identifies the decomposition-only variant, NS-Mamba-D, as a strong candidate, reducing diagnostic long-horizon RMSE by 7.48\% versus S\_Mamba and 6.80\% versus iTransformer.
After Mamba-specific hyperparameter optimization, MS-Mamba-MD becomes the strongest Mamba-family model, reaching 24.45 average RMSE over the 120/180/360-day diagnostic horizons.
This optimized Mamba result is stronger than optimized NLinear and TimesNet on the same long-horizon average, but optimized PatchTST remains best with average RMSE 23.29.
The next step is to evaluate the HPO-selected MS-Mamba-MD configuration across the full 12-horizon GLD suite.
The manuscript will become submission-ready after the proposed-model results, ablations, and broader benchmark evaluation are completed with traceable quantitative evidence.
