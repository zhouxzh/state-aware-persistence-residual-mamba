\section{Conclusion}

This manuscript presents a structured path for nonstationarity-aware Mamba forecasting.
The completed Optuna-tuned GLD baseline sweep shows, under RMSSE-based evaluation, that TimesNet is the strongest overall baseline, with iTransformer a near-tied runner-up, NLinear strongest on short horizons, PatchTST strongest on middle horizons, and TimesNet/iTransformer splitting the long-horizon wins.
The proposed NS-Mamba and Mamba architecture families combine decomposition, multi-scale temporal modeling, and Mamba-based sequence processing around an S-Mamba backbone.
After Mamba-specific Optuna search, PMS-Mamba-PD becomes the strongest completed Mamba-family model.
On the promoted four-horizon summary over 120, 180, 270, and 360 days, it reaches average RMSSE 27.25, average RMSE 34.17, and average MAE 23.72.
This optimized Mamba result is slightly stronger than the strongest Optuna-tuned non-Mamba reference on the same RMSSE summary, while the broader claim still remains limited to GLD.
The next step is to complete the broader optimized benchmark evaluation with traceable quantitative evidence beyond GLD.
