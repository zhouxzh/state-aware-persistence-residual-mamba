TimesNet is the strongest Optuna-tuned baseline overall, with balanced test RMSSE 15.66.
iTransformer is effectively tied for first, trailing by only 0.02 RMSSE points (0.14\%), while PatchTST is third with balanced test RMSSE 15.99.
The horizon-level winners show a regime split: NLinear is strongest at 1, 3, and 7 days; Non-stationary Transformer is strongest at 5 days; PatchTST leads at 14, 30, and 90 days; TimesNet leads at 60, 120, and 180 days; and iTransformer leads at 270 and 360 days.

The plain Transformer, Reformer, and Informer configurations remain far weaker on the held-out test split despite low validation objectives for some trials.
This validation-to-test mismatch is why the manuscript reports final test RMSSE and keeps the selected hyperparameters visible for exact reproduction.
