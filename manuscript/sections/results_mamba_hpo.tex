The current search changes the Mamba-family picture substantially.
MS-Mamba-MD is the strongest completed Mamba variant on 10 of the 12 horizons that currently have HPO-best rows, while S-Mamba is strongest at 5 days and MS-Mamba-MDG is strongest at 30 days.
Relative to the fixed-setting diagnostic NS-Mamba-D row, the tuned NS-Mamba-D row improves the 120/180/360 average from 26.74 to 24.83.
On the completed common long-horizon subset, MS-Mamba-MD is the strongest Mamba-family model with average RMSE 24.45 and average MAE 17.97.
On the promoted four-horizon summary 120/180/270/360, MS-Mamba-MD reaches average RMSE 25.43 and average MAE 18.64.
This is stronger than optimized NLinear (26.28) and optimized TimesNet (27.01) on the same four-horizon average, but it still trails optimized PatchTST (24.50).
The remaining gap to PatchTST is concentrated at 120 days (16.32 vs. 15.32), 180 days (21.47 vs. 21.23), 270 days (28.37 vs. 28.13), 360 days (35.55 vs. 33.33).
The corresponding relative RMSE gap between MS-Mamba-MD and PatchTST on the promoted four-horizon summary is 3.78\%.
