\section{Financial Data Analysis and Current Empirical Status}

\subsection{Observed Representation Shift}

The available evidence describes the modeling problem targeted by PMS-Mamba but does not yield a conclusion about forecast accuracy.
Across the four training targets, the level ADF test fails to reject a unit root while the level KPSS test rejects trend stationarity at its reported lower $p$-value bound (Table~\ref{tab:selection-safe-diagnostics}).
This pattern does not prove that the series share one integrated data-generating process; it shows that a common stationary-level representation is not adequate for these observed histories.

The validation period differs materially from training (Figure~\ref{fig:financial-nonstationarity}).
Foreign exchange and Treasury yields shift downward by 1.294 and 0.512 training standard deviations, whereas GLD shifts upward by 1.708 training standard deviations.
BTCUSDT exhibits the largest changes: its validation mean is 7.750 training standard deviations above the training mean, its level standard deviation is 3.669 times the training value, and its first-difference standard deviation is 4.521 times the training value.
The range of volatility changes is therefore not reducible to one global rescaling rule.

Cross-series structure also changes between training and validation.
The largest absolute change in a feature--target first-difference correlation is 0.268 for foreign exchange, 0.026 for yields, 0.323 for cross assets, and 0.106 for cryptoassets.
This variation supports evaluating target-only and multivariate inputs separately: additional variables may be informative in one panel or period but cannot be assumed to help uniformly.

The correct empirical question is not whether one Mamba label has the smallest isolated point estimate.
It is whether temporal patching, decomposition, and multi-scale pooling each contribute beyond matched alternatives, and whether any contribution persists against last-value and statistical references across panels, horizons, and volatility regimes.
No such conclusion is possible from the current evidence.

\subsection{Recomputation Plan for the Model Claims}

The next calculation cycle will preserve the chronological splits and final-period gate, but will replace the current partial comparison with a mechanism-oriented ablation matrix.
It will include a target-only variable-token base, a patch-only model, a decomposition-only model, a multi-scale-only model, decomposition-plus-multi-scale variants, and full PMS-Mamba with matched parameter counts and the same selection budget.
For each task and horizon, the final report will include path and terminal RMSSE, last-value skill, five-seed uncertainty, origin-level moving-block bootstrap intervals, and panel-specific failures as well as improvements.

The paired S/MS analysis will remain separate from the architectural ablation: it tests the incremental predictive value of the supplied panel histories, not the mechanism of PMS-Mamba.
Return forecasting, if later added, will be labelled as a robustness analysis and cannot replace a level-forecast result.
