It remains stronger than the shared locked-setting baselines and the other completed NS-Mamba diagnostic variants, but it is now clearly outperformed by the stronger completed Mamba-HPO rows as well as the best completed imported optimized baselines.
This comparison clarifies the role of the diagnostic ablation: the fixed-setting decomposition-only result is a useful proof of concept, but the strongest current Mamba-family performance comes after additional tuning of lookback length, moving-average window, and multi-scale structure.
