\section{Discussion and Limitations}

The descriptive result differs by series.
On GLD, the selected PMS-Mamba-PD point estimate is below both TimesNet and last-value, whereas persistence is lowest on Exchange.
The Exchange result is especially important for interpretation: a small neural-to-neural difference does not establish forecasting value when neither learned model improves on the last observation.
The evidence therefore supports conditional competitiveness on one target period rather than a general Mamba advantage.

The present test periods are not fully independent confirmation sets for the research program.
Although the final sweep implementation selects hyperparameters on validation data, earlier GLD test outcomes informed architecture promotion.
This research-cycle reuse can make the final GLD ordering optimistic and cannot be corrected through textual qualification alone.
A confirmatory study requires a newly frozen future period or an additional untouched dataset, with the model family, metric, exclusions, and analysis fixed before outcomes are inspected.

Optimization and stochastic uncertainty further limit architecture comparisons.
Exchange allocates 24 trial records per Mamba study and 20 per neural-baseline study, while GLD uses different calibration suites across families.
Search spaces and pruning rates are model-specific, and several studies retain interrupted or failed records.
Every selected configuration uses seed 2023, and CUDA execution is not guaranteed to be bitwise deterministic.
In the GLD reproduction bundle, 90 of 120 replayed neural-baseline metric vectors match the frozen table at strict numerical tolerance, while TimesNet balanced RMSSE changes from 15.66 to 15.50.
The resulting PMS-Mamba-PD reduction is 4.94\% rather than 5.89\%, illustrating why small differences require repeated training.

The test errors are also statistically dependent.
Adjacent forecast origins overlap, and every nominal length $H$ pools leads 1 through $H$; estimates at different origins, leads, and lengths are therefore not independent replications.
The 360 reported method--length cells summarize evaluation tasks rather than a sample size for inference.
Repeated seeds and paired uncertainty procedures that respect temporal dependence, such as block resampling over forecast origins or an appropriately specified loss-differential analysis, are needed before making inferential claims.

The S-mode design limits mechanism claims.
S-Mamba's inverted encoder normally scans variable tokens, but each experiment here contains only one variable token, so the bidirectional variable scan cannot express cross-variable dependence.
PMS-Mamba-PD instead scans temporal patch tokens, but the current comparison does not isolate tokenization, decomposition, parameter count, or optimization budget in a controlled ablation.
The results therefore do not demonstrate that a particular component causes an improvement.
Likewise, absent parameter, runtime, memory, and hardware records, Mamba's computational scaling is a literature motivation rather than an empirical result of this study.

Dataset scope remains narrow.
Two financial level series and one chronological split do not represent the diversity of forecasting applications, and no rolling-origin evaluation is included.
The study predicts levels rather than returns and reports statistical errors rather than direction, risk, transaction-cost-adjusted utility, or another economic outcome.
It should not be interpreted as evidence of trading profitability or market inefficiency.
Weather cannot currently broaden the evidence because its exclusion followed exploratory runs with an unhandled missing-value code; all methods must be rerun after a prespecified cleaning rule.

Finally, the Exchange extraction is reproducible from the local raw file, but the original FRED series identifier, quote convention, and retrieval record are missing.
The exact experiment hardware is also absent, and the working repository has not yet been archived as a citable release.
These provenance gaps must be resolved before a reproducibility claim extends beyond the accompanying local artifacts.
