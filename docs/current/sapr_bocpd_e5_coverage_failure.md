# BOCPD E5 Coverage Failure

**Status:** immutable pre-training failure record.

E5 was the fifth and final predeclared BOCPD exploration variant. It used a
lower hazard, longer history, and first-difference input. T6 met the paired
detector criterion (visible-minus-stable activation 0.1989), but T4 did not
(0.0012 versus the frozen 0.10 requirement). No E5 model was trained.

With E1-E5 all failing a pre-training detector-coverage gate, the BOCPD
exploration terminates without a candidate selection or an independent
confirmation matrix. No sixth variant is authorized from these outcomes.
