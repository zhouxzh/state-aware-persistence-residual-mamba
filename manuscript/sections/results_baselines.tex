Within the optimized baseline sweep, iTransformer and S-Mamba remain the two strongest directly comparable models.
iTransformer is the strongest optimized baseline for 10 horizons, covering 3, 5, 7, 30, 60, 90, 120, 180, 270, and 360 days, while S-Mamba is strongest at 1 and 14 days.
The optimized long-horizon gap is material rather than negligible: at 120 days the RMSE gap is about 13.90\% in favor of iTransformer, while at 360 days the gap is about 6.62\%.
Under model-specific lookback optimization, iTransformer is clearly the stronger middle- and long-horizon baseline among the six directly comparable backbone models.

Autoformer becomes a clear third-place baseline overall under the same optimized sweep.
However, its overall average RMSE of 16.11 remains above the two leaders, whose overall average RMSE values are 14.98 for S-Mamba and 13.77 for iTransformer.
The plain attention baselines remain substantially weaker on GLD even after lookback optimization, with overall average RMSE above 58 for Transformer and above 60 for Informer and Reformer.

The MAE pattern is similar.
S-Mamba and iTransformer remain close overall on MAE as well, with averages 10.62 and 10.00, respectively.
These optimized baseline results establish iTransformer as the strongest directly comparable baseline and S-Mamba as the strongest Mamba-style baseline prior to the broader Mamba-family search.
