\subsection{NS-Mamba Diagnostic Ablation}

The first NS-Mamba diagnostic sweep is complete for horizons 30, 120, 180, and 360 days.
This is a partial ablation rather than the final 12-horizon result table.
The completed diagnostic stage contains 16 finished runs, corresponding to four NS-Mamba variants evaluated on four horizons under the same locked Setting A used by the GLD baselines.

The strongest diagnostic variant is NS-Mamba-D, which uses series decomposition with the S\_Mamba normalization pathway and does not use adaptive normalization or shift-aware fusion.
Against the better of S\_Mamba and iTransformer at each diagnostic horizon, NS-Mamba-D reduces RMSE by 13.13\% at 30 days, 8.25\% at 120 days, 9.33\% at 180 days, and 4.33\% at 360 days.
Its long-horizon average RMSE is 26.74, compared with 28.90 for S\_Mamba and 28.68 for iTransformer.
This corresponds to a 7.49\% reduction versus S\_Mamba and a 6.79\% reduction versus iTransformer.
The diagnostic result is also informative because the more complex variants do not improve the metric.
Adaptive normalization alone is weaker than S\_Mamba on the diagnostic long horizons, and adding adaptive normalization to the decomposition branch weakens the decomposition-only result.
The shift-aware gate also does not help in this first diagnostic sweep.
Therefore, the next experiment should promote NS-Mamba-D to the full 12-horizon GLD evaluation before adding further complexity.
