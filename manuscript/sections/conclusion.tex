\section{Conclusion}

This manuscript presents a structured path for nonstationarity-aware Mamba forecasting.
The completed optimized GLD baseline sweep shows that iTransformer is the strongest overall directly comparable backbone model, while S-Mamba remains the strongest Mamba-style baseline before broader Mamba-family tuning.
The completed source-optimized imported sweep further shows that NLinear is strongest at short horizons and PatchTST is strongest overall among the tuned imported baselines.
The proposed NS-Mamba and MS-Mamba architecture families combine decomposition, multi-scale temporal modeling, and Mamba-based sequence processing around an S-Mamba backbone.
After Mamba-specific hyperparameter optimization, MS-Mamba-MD becomes the strongest completed Mamba-family model.
On the promoted four-horizon summary over 120, 180, 270, and 360 days, it reaches average RMSE 25.43 and average MAE 18.64.
This optimized Mamba result is stronger than optimized NLinear and TimesNet on the same four-horizon average, but optimized PatchTST remains best with average RMSE 24.50.
The next step is to complete the broader optimized benchmark evaluation with traceable quantitative evidence beyond GLD and beyond the currently uneven cross-variant coverage.
